How to Play Bordle

Welcome to Bordle! Here's how to play:

  • Guess the 5-letter word in 6 tries or less.
  • Each guess must be a valid 5-letter word.
  • After each guess, the color of the tiles will change to show how close your guess was to the word.

Color Meanings

  • Green: The letter is in the word and in the correct position.
  • Yellow: The letter is in the word but in the wrong position.
  • Gray: The letter is not in the word.

Tips for Success

  • It is suggested to have 2 words to start off with that covers as many vowls and letters as possible.
  • This allows you to gather more infomration about the word and helps narrow down the answer extremely quickly
